# Margin strategy, July 2026

## Decision and reader

This document governs Margin work through October 11, 2026. It is written for Sam and for an executor arriving without portfolio context. Margin remains the portfolio's first investment and a candidate user product. External use now decides whether it earns a continued user-product roadmap. Decision provenance lives in `~/Projects/PORTFOLIO.md`, and current stage state lives in `~/.claude/skills/data/foundry.json`.

## Position

Margin turns a writer's corrections into enforceable rules that improve the next AI-assisted draft.

The first external user is a product leader or job seeker who drafts high-stakes professional prose with Claude Code, notices generic AI patterns, and already corrects those patterns by hand. Sam will choose two people from his network and record their names in the session log before any new engineering task starts.

## Current evidence

Sam has used Margin deeply, with 364 authored commits in the last year and 32 in the last 30 days. The correction, synthesis, review, export, and guard loop exists. The latest public release is v1.16.2; the v1.17.0 release workflow failed during notarization on July 6. External usage remains unproven.

The July distribution fence failed. Feature work continued after the strategy called for distribution, while completed external sessions stayed at zero. That behavior is now a portfolio-level control failure.

## Shipped and being used

Margin counts as shipped and used when both selected writers complete a real correction-to-rule session in the current release. Each session must produce one accepted rule visible in the writing profile generated by `margin export profile`. An executor verifies it by running that command and finding the accepted rule in the generated profile. At least one writer must then apply the profile to a second draft and say whether the output improved.

Sam's daily use remains valuable product evidence. It cannot answer whether another writer understands the loop or returns to it.

## Product fence

Until both sessions finish, an executor may only fix a release blocker or a defect that prevents the observed correction-to-rule task. Every allowed fix must cite the affected session and its failed step.

Marketplace packaging, onboarding expansion, capture intelligence, new AI surfaces, design-audit enhancements, collaboration, cloud sync, monetization, and architecture work are out of scope. Opening any other feature branch requires a new Founder decision that explicitly ends this fence.

The CPO challenger blocked further feature work and classified Margin as a candidate user product until two sessions finish. The Seed VC kept the option alive only through those sessions and required return use within fourteen days. The Founder decision is to keep Margin first in the slate while tying its next status change to external behavior. Build volume and technical depth no longer count as progress on the product question.

Both challengers also tested the feature fence. The controlling rule is stricter than the July version because the earlier fence was breached. Only release blockers and observed-session defects may enter execution before both sessions finish.

## Stop conditions

Convert Margin to a portfolio artifact if any of these conditions occurs.

- The release is not send-ready by August 15.
- A validator invitation remains unsent seven days after the public build is ready.
- No external session finishes by September 3.
- Neither validator uses the profile on a second draft within fourteen days of the final session.
- Work outside the product fence resumes without a Founder decision.

Conversion preserves the app, repository, release, and case study. It ends the user-product roadmap and all standing feature obligations.
