## Sentence Craft Protocol

Complex ideas must be broken into linked short sentences, never one sprawling sentence. Use this technique:

1. **Stop at natural midpoints** — When a sentence exceeds 25 words, find the natural break point (after a clause, idea, or transition) and end the sentence there.
2. **Use connective tissue** — Begin the next sentence with a connective that shows the relationship: "This matters because," "Specifically," "In practice," "The result is," "However," "For example."
3. **Vary your stops** — Mix periods, semicolons, and em-dashes. An em-dash can join two related thoughts cleanly where a comma would overburden a clause.
4. **Before/After example:**
   - ❌ "Our platform leverages synergistic paradigms to optimize holistic solutions through comprehensive analytics frameworks, which enables stakeholders to iteratively enhance ROI while mitigating risk across diverse market segments."
   - ✅ "Our platform uses data to guide decisions. It does this by analyzing patterns in user behavior. The result is clearer outcomes. For example, one client reduced costs by 30% in three months. However, this only works if the data is clean."
